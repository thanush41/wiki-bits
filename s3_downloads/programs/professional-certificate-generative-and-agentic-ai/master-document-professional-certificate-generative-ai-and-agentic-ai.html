Professional Certificate in Generative & Agentic AI | Updated for September/October 2026 Cohort

1. PROGRAM OVERVIEW

 

Program Objective

This program is designed to help working technology professionals move from using Generative AI tools to building production-like Generative AI and Agentic AI applications. The ~30-week curriculum focuses on practical implementation, technical foundations, and application-building capability across modern AI systems.

The curriculum focuses on:

  • Large Language Models (LLMs)
  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
  • AI agents and agentic workflows
  • Evaluation and deployment of AI systems
  • Production-like readiness
  • Responsible and governed use of AI systems
  • Capstone-based demonstration of real AI work

3. ELIGIBILITY RULES

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants seeking admission to the Professional Certificate in Generative & Agentic AI must meet the following eligibility requirements:

Foundational Proficiency Requirements

Applicants are expected to have foundational proficiency in the following areas:

  • Python programming
  • AWS fundamentals

Applicants should be familiar with the topics listed in the foundational proficiency document: [Link to Foundational Proficiency Topics here]

Work Experience

Applicants must have a minimum of 2 years of relevant work experience in software development, technology, data, analytics, cloud, AI/ML, IT, or other allied domains.

Educational Qualification

Applicants must possess a Bachelor’s degree from a recognised university, with Mathematics as a part of the curriculum.

Self-Declaration of Foundational Proficiency

Applicants must submit a self-declaration confirming that they have foundational proficiency in Python and AWS as part of the application form.

Bridge Pathway for Applicants Without Foundational Proficiency

Applicants who do not currently meet the foundational proficiency requirements in Python and AWS will have the option to complete an 8-week Bridge Program covering these two areas. Upon successful completion of the Bridge Program, they may proceed to the Professional Certificate in Generative & Agentic AI, subject to batch availability and approval of the Institute.
