M.Sc in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence - Eligibility

 

Indian Applicants

Educational background

You must have a bachelor's degree with Math or Statistics as one of your subjects, and an overall score of 60% or above.

Age Requirement

There’s no age limit — if you’re ready to learn, we’re ready for you.

English Proficiency

Since the program is taught in English, you’ll need to be comfortable with the language.

Currently enrolled in a STEM program?

If you’re pursuing a postgraduate degree in science or engineering that includes Math, that works too. You can apply while you're still studying.

Work Experience

You don’t need any prior work experience to apply. Just bring your curiosity and commitment.

International Applicants

Educational background

You must have a 3-year college degree with Math as a subject, from a recognised university. A minimum 60% marks or 6.0 CGPA overall aggregate is required. If your degree is from outside India, an equivalence certificate from University Grants Commission/UGC equivalence certificate or a similar Indian body is needed.

Age Requirement

There’s no age limit — if you’re ready to learn, we’re ready for you.

English Proficiency

Since the program is taught in English, you’ll need to be comfortable with the language.

Work Experience

You don’t need any prior work experience to apply. Just bring your curiosity and commitment.
