INTENT 1: Batch Size / Student Strength 

User Variations: 

“How many students are enrolled in this program?”  

“What is the batch size?”  

“Will students be divided into smaller batches?”  

Response: 

The batch size may vary across cohorts. The programme is designed to scale while maintaining quality through structured delivery, including: 

Faculty-led sessions  

Teaching assistants (TAs)  

Discussion forums and interactive components  

For certain academic activities such as projects and discussions, students may be grouped to ensure better engagement and learning outcomes. 

 

Question2: Faculty Interaction / Student Support at Scale 

Variations: 

“What is the professor-student ratio?”  

“How will doubts be handled with so many students?”  

“Will I get individual attention?”  

Response: 

The programme is designed to ensure structured and accessible academic support. 

You will have access to: 

Faculty interaction during live sessions  

Teaching assistants (TAs) for doubt resolution  

Discussion forums for continuous engagement  

This ensures that your queries are addressed in a timely and structured manner. 

 

Question3: ABC Credits (Academic Bank of Credits) 

Variations: 

“Will my credits be added to ABC?”  

“Is this programme aligned with ABC?”  

Response: 

Yes, the programme is aligned with UGC guidelines, and credits earned may be integrated with the Academic Bank of Credits (ABC) framework. 

The detailed process and timelines for credit reflection are shared during your academic journey. 

 

Question4: IGNITE Cell – Career Support Structure 

Variations: 

“What is IGNITE Cell?”  

“How is it different from campus placements?”  

“Is IGNITE internal or external?”  

“How does IGNITE help students?”  

Response: 

The IGNITE Cell is the dedicated career support and placement assistance team for BITS Pilani Digital programmes. 

It provides: 

Career guidance and mentorship  

Resume building and interview preparation  

Access to job and internship opportunities (where applicable)  

Unlike traditional campus placements, IGNITE functions as a structured support system, helping you prepare and connect with opportunities. Outcomes depend on performance, skills, and market conditions.

Variations:

“How will internship work if I am working?”
