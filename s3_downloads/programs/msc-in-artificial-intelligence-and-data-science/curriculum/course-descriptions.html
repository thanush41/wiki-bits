M.Sc. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence - Course Descriptions

 

Statistical Modelling and Inferencing (3 Credits)

Probability Distributions; Statistical Inference; Analysis of variance; Regression and Correlation; Discriminant analysis; Factor Analysis; Cluster Analysis; Time series analysis; Forecasting; Nonparametric methods; Unbiasedness; Consistency; Uniform minimum variance unbiased estimator; Method of moments; Maximum likelihood estimation and its properties

Machine Learning (3 Credits)

Notion of loss functions and optimization algorithms in ML; Supervised learning (Linear Regression, Logistic Regression, Naive Bayes, Decision Trees, kNN, SVM, Ensembles), Unsupervised learning (clustering, k-means, Association Rule Mining, applications), ML model training & performance evaluation, Hyperparameter tuning.

Data Preprocessing (2 Credits)

Types of data, datasets, properties (including statistical properties) of data, transformations on data; Data sources, challenges, quality issues; Data cleaning, imputation, preprocessing; Notion of dimensionality and proximity.

Data Visualization & Storytelling (3 Credits)

Exploratory vs. Explanatory analysis; Understanding the context for data presentations in 2D and 3D; Effective visuals; Gestalt principles of visual perception; Visual ordering, Decluttering, Storytelling; Visualization Design; Taxonomy of data visualization methods; Bullet graphs, Pareto charts; Dashboard categorization, Characteristics of a well-designed dashboard and Key goals in the visual design process; Techniques for visualization of high dimensional data & Discovered patterns; Effective Dashboard Display Media, Designing Dashboards for Usability; Common mistakes in Dashboards.

Feature Engineering (2 Credits)

Feature engineering (feature transformation, feature creation, dimensionality reduction - PCA, SVD, feature subset selection), correlation analysis; Preprocessing and feature engineering techniques for text, image and time-series data; Techniques for combining datasets from multiple sources (e.g., joining, merging); Automated feature engineering.

Data Stores and Pipelines (3 Credits)

Databases, data warehouses, data lakes; Usage of data infrastructure, ELT vs ETL, modern data formats (iceberg format, hudi format, delta lake format), build standard pipeline, data ingestion, scripting, CI/CD and automation in data pipeline.

Artiﬁcial Neural Networks (3 Credits)

Evolution of neural networks; ANNs and how they work - notion of activation functions, non-linearity, backward and forward propagation; Training neural networks - batch processing, and stochastic gradient descent (SGD); Regularization in ANNs; CNNs, RNNs and architectures; Popular neural network architectures (ResNet, VGG16, Inception-v3, MobileNet, EfﬁcientNet); Model evaluation; Using pre-trained NN models

Deep Learning (3 Credits)

Introduction to Deep Neural Networks and Learning from Data; Sequential Data Preparation; Data, Model Weights, Bias, and Errors in Deep Learning Framework; Deep Leaning using CNNs; Tokenization of Sequential Data; DNN for Seq2Seq Modelling; Representation Learning; Multi-task Learning using DNNs; Different Loss Functions; Familiarization with Latest Deep Neural Models available for Vision-ad-Language Modelling in HuggingFace; End-to-End Deep Learning Framework using Pytorch-Lightning; GPU Compute Units for Deep Learning

ML Model Engineering (3 Credits)

Model inferencing, model serving strategies; Deployment pipelines; Model monitoring; Continuous training/retraining, automation in ML pipeline; Trade-offs and factors that inﬂuence model training and deployment choices; Model standardization; Model compression

Natural Language Processing and Understanding (3 Credits)

Introduction to NLP and NLU Concepts; Introduction to Morphology of written Natural Texts; Basics of Text Analysis; Overview of Text Corpora; Text Analysis and Manipulation; Natural Language Understanding; NLP and NLU with LLMs.

Fundamentals of Finance (3 Credits)

Capital markets; ﬁnancial intermediation; ﬁnancial securities; risk and return; asset valuation; portfolio management; derivatives and risk management; ﬁnancial data analysis using Python

Big Data Platforms & Analytics (2 Credits)

Constraints and Issues in Processing BigData. Distributed Processing of Data. MapReduce model - Processing in disks vs. In-memory processing (e.g. Hadoop vs. Spark). Distributed Data Structures - RDDs. Resilience - Checkpointing vs. Lineage, Distributed Checkpointing. Distributed Clustering and Data Partitioning - Data Structures for Partitioning. Distributed Training of Models - Distributed Stochastic Gradient Descent, Case study - TensorFlow Architecture. Stream Processing Platforms (e.g. Storm and Kafka) and Architecture.

Optimization Methods for Analytics (3 Credits)

Linear programming; Unconstrained optimization; Quadratic programming; Non-linear programming; Integer programming; Applications to logistics, manufacturing, transportation, marketing, project management and ﬁnance. 

Principles of Marketing (3 Credits)

Introduction to the fundamentals of marketing; Marketing mix, Developing marketing strategies and plans; Capturing marketing insight; Analysing the macro-environment, Forecasting and demand measurement, Conducting marketing research; Achieving customer satisfaction and creating long-term loyalty relationships; Maximizing customer lifetime value; Analysing consumer markets; Key psychological processes; Buying decision process; Behavioural decision theory; Introduction to organizational buying; Identifying market segments and targets; Basics of brand equity; Devising a branding strategy; Competitive strategies; Product lifecycle marketing strategies; Setting product strategy; Product-service continuum; Managing service quality and achieving excellence; Developing pricing strategies; Designing and managing integrated marketing channels; E-commerce and M-commerce marketing practices, Managing retailing, wholesaling and logistics; Designing and managing integrated marketing communication

AI & ML Applications in Finance (3 Credits)

Digital economy, digital identity; Role of cloud computing, big data and blockchain in the ﬁntech ecosystem; Financial services disruptions (asset servicing, capital markets, investment management, insurance); Data sourcing for analytical models; AI applications in ﬁnance, machine learning in ﬁnance, text mining, generative AI, ﬁnancial market analysis; Regulatory technology (RegTech); Open banking, Bank 4.0; Financial sector transformation.

Software Engineering Practices (3 Credits)

Introduction to Software Engineering; Requirements Engineering; Software Design and Architecture – tools and technologies for design and architectures; Software Development Practices; Software Quality Assurance; Emerging Trends in Software Engineering: Use of AI in Software Engineering; Run-time proﬁle data and analytics; Refactoring Techniques

Distributed Data Systems (3 Credits)

Distributed Systems, Clusters - Sharding; Massive Datasets - Distributed Processing of Massive Datasets, MapReduce Frameworks - Concurrency Performance, Implementation Pragmatics, Iterative and Recursive Map-Reduce Processing, Resilience, Checkpointing; Distributed Databases/ NoSQL Databases. Design of Distributed Databases - CAP Theorem and its Implications; Strong vs. Weak Consistency - Eventual Consistency, Paxos protocol, Chubby Locking. Data Replication, Persistence, and Data Movement issues - Bandwidth and Latency, Caching, Buffering, and Copy-on-write.

Data Annotations and Labelling (3 Credits)

Relationship between Data and Annotations; Annotation Formatting; Annotation for Visual Recognition Systems; Image Captioning; Video Captioning; Foundational LLMs ans VLMs as Human like annotators; Annotation Quality Measure; Familiarization with Labelling Tools;

Analytics for Retail Management (3 Credits)

Introduction to retailing; Types of retailing; Customer buying behaviour; Retailing strategy; Information System and supply chain management; Merchandize management; Human resources and store management; Introduction to retail analytics; Customer Data Mining; Market basket analysis; Tools for analysing retail valuation; Managing customer base; Segmenting customer; Customer value analysis; Estimating customer life time value; Customer churn analysis.

Marketing Analytics (3 Credits)

Introduction to marketing analytics; Pricing of single and bundled Products; Forecasting sales of a new product; Conjoint analysis; Using logistic regression for customer selection; Discrete Choice Analysis / Discriminant Analysis; Using Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) Analysis for customer selection; Using customer lifetime value for customer selection; Market segmentation; Cluster Analysis; Market Basket Analysis, Media mix model

Analytics for E-Commerce (3 Credits)

e-Commerce analytics value chain; Methods and techniques for e-Commerce analysis; Visualizing, dashboarding, and reporting e-Commerce data and analysis; e-Commerce analytics data model and technology; Marketing and advertising analytics in e-Commerce; Analysing behavioural data; Optimizing for e-Commerce conversion and user experience; Analysing e-Commerce customers; Analysing products and orders in e-Commerce; Attribution in e-Commerce analytics; Integrating data and analysis to drive your e-Commerce strategy

Digital Marketing Strategies & Analytics (3 Credits)

Fundamentals of digital marketing; Digital marketing models and frameworks; Digital marketing strategy; Stepping into digital analytics; Clickstream analysis, Social media analytics; Measuring the success of blogs; Mobile analytics; Competitive analysis; Social media analytics; Measuring the success of blogs; Mobile analytics; Return on investment; Competitive intelligence analysis

Supply Chain Analytics (3 Credits)

Introduction to supply chain analytics; Overview of optimization methods; Facility locations and discrete location models; Inventory concepts, costs and basic models; Inventory under uncertainty and service levels; Trade-off decisions and network models in transportation; Routing using the traveling salesman problem algorithms in transportation; Routing and scheduling problems and methods; Multi-criteria decision making

Applied Financial Analytics – I (3 Credits)

Ratio analysis; discounted cash ﬂow analysis; bond valuation; mean-variance portfolio analysis; portfolio optimization; capital budgeting;

Applied Financial Analytics – II (3 Credits)

Modelling using Financial Statements Data; Modelling stock price behaviour; Modern portfolio theory; Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM); Arbitrage Pricing Theory (APT), and efﬁcient market theory; Passive investing; Active investing; Portfolio management including allocation, rebalancing and risk management; Valuation of equity securities, ﬁxed income securities, and derivatives; Efﬁcient portfolio models and evaluation; Monte Carlo simulation; Advanced risk analysis and Trading strategies.

Pricing Analytics (3 Credits)

Introduction to the theory of pricing; Role of elasticity in pricing; Pricing for different customer segments; Break-even analysis; Empirical estimation of price-response functions; Price optimization and markdown optimization; Hedonic pricing model; Revenue management; Conjoint Analysis for pricing decisions; Legal and ethical issues

Financial Risk Analytics – I (3 Credits)

Credit functions and credit products in banks/ ﬁnancial institutions; Credit risk concepts/ terminology (Default, charge-off, bankruptcy, loan restructuring, loan moratorium); Credit risk identiﬁcation, measurement (probability of default, exposure at default and loss given default); Credit score cards (retail), rating models (corporate), Counterparty credit risk and Securitization of loan portfolios; Regulatory framework for Credit Risk - Basel norms; Credit risk analytics - Value at Risk (Credit), Risk adjusted return on capital, Economic capital; Model risk management - Regulatory reporting.

Financial Risk Analytics – II (3 Credits)

Non-Credit products (investments - bonds/ equity, forex, derivatives) and services (non-interest income and fee-based products); Market, Operation, and Liquidity risk concepts and terminology - market to market, trading versus banking book, business indicators approach, internal loss models, liquidity coverage and net stable funding ratios; Regulatory guidelines - BASEL norms; Market risk measurement and modelling - Volatility, VaR, Stressed VaR, Expected shortfall; Pricing of derivatives, forex products; Interest Rate Risk in Banking Book; Fundamental review of trading book; Operational risk modelling- business indicator/ standardized approaches, internal loss multiplier; Liquidity risk modelling - Run-off factors, scenario modelling, liquidity optimization; Stress testing, regulatory reporting and model risk management

Management of Banks and Financial Institutions (3 Credits)

Overview and operation of ﬁnancial institutions and banks; Commercial banking, Investment banking; Deﬁne, quantify, and manage various types of risks faced by ﬁnancial institutions; asset liability management - overview, strategies; off balance sheet activities of banks; sovereign risk; deposit insurance; capital adequacy; study current best practices using selected case studies; bank management failures; insights from collapse of Lehman Brothers; introduction to bank regulation; international considerations; relevant case studies, simulations, modelling.

Data Annotations and Labelling (3 Credits)

Relationship between Data and Annotations; Annotation Formatting; Annotation for Visual Recognition Systems; Image Captioning; Video Captioning; Foundational LLMs and VLMs as Human like annotators; Annotation Quality Measure; Familiarization with Labelling Tools;

Analytics for Retail Management (Common course with Retail/ e-commerce) (3 Credits)

Retail business’s business intelligence environment, Retail information systems, Data-driven tools and theoretical analytical models, Analytical tools for retail, Statistical methods in econometrics and machine learning like -single and multivariate linear regressions, logistic regressions, and classiﬁcation trees, merchandising analytics, Customer analytics, Retail data reporting & visualization.

ML in Software Quality Assurance (3 Credits)

Introduction to ML in Software Quality Assurance; Defect Prediction and Prevention; Automated Testing with ML; ML in Code Analysis; Predictive Analytics in SQA; Resource Allocation Optimization; Tools and Frameworks for ML in SQA.

Intelligent Clinical Decision Support Systems (3 Credits)

Role, types, and evolution of clinical decision support systems (CDSS) in healthcare, key components and architecture of intelligent CDSS; Data standards like HL7, FHIR, and DICOM; preprocessing, feature extraction, and fusion techniques for multimodal data integration; Deep learning vision and language techniques for medical data analysis e.g. diagnosis, treatment planning, monitoring, and risk prediction; AI explainability and interpretability in healthcare; Ethical considerations, data privacy, and regulatory requirements needed for CDSS.

AI in Community Health (3 Credits)

Understanding of community data; Social determinants of health; data scrapping and preprocessing methods; Single variate/multivariate correlation methods; Predictive Models design and evaluation; Analysis of SDH at g data from GIS (Google Maps, Open Street Maps (OSM);

Transitioning to Intelligent Healthcare Practices (3 Credits)

Drivers and barriers of digital innovation in the healthcare ecosystem; Digital transformation of healthcare; AI applied to digital healthcare; AI and telemedicine; AI and digitization in administrative tasks; Infrastructure, regulatory, legal, workforce considerations; Potential trade-offs and consequences of AI; Deploying AI in clinical settings; Software ecosystem for AI in healthcare; Role of the policy environment, e.g., Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission

Pipeline Orchestration - Tools and Technologies; Containers (e.g.lxc containers, and Dockers), Container Orchestration (e.g. Kubernetes); Model Optimization - Speculated Decoding, Flash Attention, KV Caching; Model Quantization, Quantization, API Integration, and Quantization Tools; Model Serving Platforms and Model Serving; Model Telemetry; MLOps

Introduction to Evolutionary Computation, Genetic Algorithms, Introduction to Swarm Intelligence - PSO, ACO, Cuckoo Search; Application to Software Engineering Problems (Case Studies) - Using Genetic Algorithms to generate inputs four boundary testing; Bug Source identiﬁcation using EA; Automating Test case generation using EA; Predicting the impact of changes in the codebase using EA-optimized models, Using EAs to resolve conﬂicts during version control merges, Optimizing how APIs are called or combined for speciﬁc tasks, Automating the tuning of CI/CD pipelines for efﬁciency and reliability, Application of EAs for Optimizing agile workﬂows, Automating feature testing for mobile and web applications.

Low-code / No-code Application Development; Application Design and Development using Micro-services; Common Cloud-Provider Services for ML and GenAI applications (e.g. AWS and Azure APIs): Cloud-Native Services, Infrastructure, and Deployment resources; Cloud-native APIs for GenAI Applications; Rest APIs for ML; Deploying ML models as serverless APIs.

GenAI -based Products (3 Credits)

Generative AI and NLP capabilities. NLP Products - Conversational AI products, Speech-based Products; VLM-based products; Multimodal Interfaces; Agentic AI and Embedding GenAI in small Form-Factor devices and consumer products (e.g. smart-phones, cars, etc.)

SaaS and micro-services architecture; Deploying Data Science, ML, and GenAI applications as Cloud Services; SaaS costing and Pricing - Data Intensive Applications, ML, applications, and GenAI applications; Service Requests and handling requests. SaaS Business Models for Data Science, AI, and ML.

AI Product Management (3 Credits)

Customization of Generative AI for speciﬁc domains - Retraining approaches, domain ﬁtting, evaluating RAG, CAG, etc,, cost of evals, tradeoff - accuracy vs. (cost of ) evals vs. governance/compliance.; Buy, vs. Build, vs. Customize decisions; AI as UI; ML models - training, retraining, and deployment costs; tradeoff - training and testing cost vs model performance, deployment testing and user feedback vs governance parameters; model deployment cost vs. request serving cost; Pricing parameters - data cost, cloud cost, etc; Managing performance vs explainability.
