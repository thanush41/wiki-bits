M.Sc. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence - Examination Information

 

Students in India

Students residing in India will take their exams in person at one of BITS Pilani’s officially designated examination centres.

Exam Format & Schedule

Trimester end exams for each course are typically scheduled on Fridays, Saturdays, or Sundays. For each course, students can choose between two available date and time options for trimester end exams—making it easier to plan around personal and professional commitments.

Designated Exam Centres

Choose from 22 centres spread across major cities in four zones:

SOUTH ZONE: Bangalore, Chennai, Hyderabad, Vijayawada, Thiruvananthapuram

NORTH ZONE: Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Pilani, Lucknow, Dehradun, Chandigarh, Jammu

WEST ZONE: Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur, Goa, Ahmedabad

EAST ZONE: Kolkata, Bhubaneswar, Patna,Jamshedpur, Guwahati

Students may update their preferred exam centre each trimester, subject to availability.

Students Outside India

Students residing outside India will take their exams in an online proctored format from their country of residence.

Exam Format & Requirements

Exams are conducted with remote proctoring to ensure academic integrity.

Students must ensure access to:

- A computer/laptop meeting prescribed specifications

- High-speed, stable internet

- A quiet, private space free from disruptions during the exam period

All requirements and technical guidelines will be shared in advance to ensure readiness.
