2. Registration & Fee Rules 

Each trimester begins with mandatory registration. 
 
Registration is complete only when: 
- Student confirms course package 
- Full trimester fee is paid 
 
Trimester fee remains fixed irrespective of number of courses registered. 
 
AI must not imply fee reduction for fewer courses. 

3. Eligibility Logic (Postgraduate) 

Eligibility requires: 
 
- Bachelor’s degree (minimum 3 years) from recognised university 
- Minimum 60% aggregate OR 6.0 CGPA (10-point scale) 
- At least one subject in Mathematics or Statistics at bachelor’s level 
 
No age limit. 
Work experience not mandatory. 
 
AI must: 
- Validate eligibility logically 
- Avoid guaranteeing admission 
- Escalate international equivalence cases.

7. Maximum Duration Rule 

Maximum duration to complete full 2-year M.Sc.: 4 years. 
 
No additional fee for slower pace. 
Continuity fee applies only if trimester drop taken.

8. Trimester Drop & Continuity Fee 

Break allowed only before trimester begins. 
 
Continuity Fee: ₹10,000 per trimester of absence. 
 
Break requires Institute approval. 
Continuity fee payable at time of re-registration. 
 
AI must:

- Not override approval requirement.
