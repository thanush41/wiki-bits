M.Sc. in Data Science & AI – Academic Preparedness Programmes

Knowledge Base for AI Chatbot, Voice Bot & Admissions Counselors

Overview

BITS Pilani Digital offers two optional academic refresher programmes for eligible applicants to the M.Sc. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence programme:

1. Mathematics Preparedness Programme

2. Programming Preparedness Programme

These programmes are designed to help learners strengthen foundational skills before commencing the M.Sc. programme.

They are not degree programmes, certificate programmes, admission pathways, or bridge programmes.

Important Clarification

The Preparedness Programmes are optional academic refresher programmes.

Applicants who meet the eligibility criteria for the M.Sc. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence programme and have received an admission offer may choose either of the following:

  • Join the M.Sc. programme directly, or
  • Complete one or more Preparedness Programmes before joining the next available M.Sc. intake.

The decision rests entirely with the learner.

Why are the Preparedness Programmes Offered?

The M.Sc. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence programme is academically rigorous and requires readiness in areas such as:

  • Mathematics
  • Statistics
  • Programming
  • Data Analysis
  • Machine Learning
  • Artificial Intelligence

To support learner success, BITS Pilani Digital offers optional refresher programmes that allow learners to revisit foundational concepts before beginning postgraduate coursework.

Mathematics Preparedness Programme

Purpose

Applicants are expected to have a solid grasp of undergraduate-level mathematics, including:

  • Probability
  • Statistics
  • Linear Algebra
  • Calculus

While graduates who have studied Mathematics or Statistics and meet the programme eligibility criteria may join the M.Sc. programme directly, some applicants may wish to refresh these concepts before beginning postgraduate coursework.

The Mathematics Preparedness Programme is designed to support such learners.

Duration

Approximately 4 months

Fee

₹8,250

Who Should Consider It?

Applicants who:

  • Come from a non-engineering background
  • Have not studied mathematics recently
  • Wish to refresh mathematical concepts
  • Prefer additional preparation before commencing the M.Sc. programme

Programming Preparedness Programme

Purpose

Applicants are expected to have basic proficiency in at least one programming language, preferably Python.

While applicants meeting the M.Sc. eligibility criteria may join directly, some learners may benefit from strengthening their programming skills before beginning the programme.

The Programming Preparedness Programme is designed to support such learners.

Duration

Approximately 4 months

Fee

₹8,500

Who Should Consider It?

Applicants who:

  • Have little or no prior programming experience
  • Have limited exposure to coding
  • Wish to strengthen computational thinking and coding skills
  • Prefer additional preparation before commencing the M.Sc. programme

Can I Take Both Programmes?

Yes.

Applicants may choose:

  • Mathematics Preparedness Programme only
  • Programming Preparedness Programme only
  • Both Mathematics and Programming Preparedness Programmes
  • Neither and proceed directly to the M.Sc. programme

If I Choose Both Programmes, What Will the Fee Be?

  • Mathematics Preparedness Programme: ₹8,250
  • Programming Preparedness Programme: ₹8,500

Total

₹16,750

Is This a Pathway to Admission?

No.

The Preparedness Programmes are not admission pathways and should not be viewed as bridge programmes leading to admission.

Admission to the M.Sc. programme is evaluated independently through the standard admissions process.

The Preparedness Programmes are optional academic refresher programmes intended to strengthen readiness for postgraduate study.

Can I Join the M.Sc. Programme Directly?

Yes.

All applicants who meet the eligibility criteria and receive an admission offer may choose to join the M.Sc. programme directly without enrolling in a Preparedness Programme.

What Happens if I Choose a Preparedness Programme?

If you choose to enroll:

  1. You will receive an offer for the selected Preparedness Programme.
  2. You will complete the Preparedness Programme first.
  3. You will join the next available M.Sc. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence intake after completion.

Can I Study the Preparedness Programme and M.Sc. Trimester 1 Together?

Yes.

Students can choose to complete the Preparedness Programmes either concurrently alongside M.Sc. Trimester 1 or sequentially, one after the other.

Learners opting for a Preparedness Programme first must complete the programme before commencing Trimester 1 of the M.Sc. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence programme.

When Will I Pay My M.Sc. Programme Fees?

Applicants opting for a Preparedness Programme will first complete the admissions and fee requirements applicable to the Preparedness Programme.

The M.Sc. admission fee and trimester fee become payable when the learner commences the M.Sc. programme after completing the refresher programme.

Is Enrollment in a Preparedness Programme Mandatory?

No.

The Preparedness Programmes are optional.

Applicants who have received an admission offer to the M.Sc. programme may either:

  • Join the M.Sc. programme directly, or
  • Choose one or both Preparedness Programmes before commencing the M.Sc. programme.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does a Preparedness Programme Mean I Am Not Eligible for the M.Sc. Programme?

No.

Preparedness Programmes are optional academic refresher programmes available to eligible applicants.

They are intended to strengthen readiness and are not an indication that an applicant has been rejected.

Who Decides Whether I Should Take a Preparedness Programme?

The learner decides.

BITS Pilani Digital provides the advisories and preparedness options, while applicants may self-assess their readiness and choose the path most appropriate for them.

Should I Take the Mathematics Preparedness Programme?

Applicants are expected to have familiarity with undergraduate-level mathematics, including probability, statistics, linear algebra, and calculus.

If you have not studied these topics recently or feel that you would benefit from a refresher before beginning the M.Sc. programme, you may consider the Mathematics Preparedness Programme.

Should I Take the Programming Preparedness Programme?

Applicants are expected to have basic proficiency in at least one programming language, preferably Python.

If you have limited exposure to programming or wish to strengthen your coding fundamentals before beginning the M.Sc. programme, you may consider the Programming Preparedness Programme.

Can I Take Both Programmes?

Yes.

Applicants may choose to undertake both the Mathematics Preparedness Programme and the Programming Preparedness Programme before joining the M.Sc. programme.

Will I Receive a Separate Degree or Certificate?

The Preparedness Programmes are academic refresher programmes and should not be represented as separate degree programmes.

Any completion documentation, if applicable, is governed by University policy.
