5. ELIGIBILITY RULES

Official Eligibility

Applicants seeking admission to the Professional Certificate in AI Engineering & MLOps must satisfy the following requirements.

Educational Qualification

Applicants must possess a Bachelor's degree from a recognised university, with Mathematics as part of the curriculum.

Work Experience

Applicants must have a minimum of 2 years of relevant work experience in software development, technology, data, analytics, cloud, AI/ML, IT, or other allied domains.

Foundational Proficiency

Applicants are expected to possess foundational proficiency in:

  • Python Programming
  • AWS Fundamentals
  • Mathematics Essentials for AI

Applicants should be familiar with the topics listed in the Foundational Proficiency document.

Self-Declaration

Applicants must submit a self-declaration confirming foundational proficiency in Python Programming, AWS Fundamentals, and Mathematics Essentials for AI.

8. LEARNING METHODOLOGY

Core Learning Model

The programme follows a flipped-classroom methodology, where learners first build conceptual understanding through self-paced learning material.

Wherever applicable, learners complete practice exercises and labs before attending periodic live sessions, scheduled mostly on weekends.

The live sessions are designed for instructor interaction, doubt resolution, deeper discussion, and augmented learning.

Weekly Live Instructor Interactions

Learners attend weekly live sessions of approximately 2 hours, mostly on weekends.

These sessions are led by subject matter experts, many of whom are experienced industry practitioners.

Support Through Teaching Assistants

Learning support extends beyond live sessions through Teaching Assistants and discussion forums.

Learners may seek conceptual clarification, guidance on practice exercises, and support on labs wherever applicable.

9. WEEKLY LEARNING STRUCTURE

Weekly Learning Rhythm

Learner effort is expected to be typically 9–10 hours per week, including:

  • Self-paced learning
  • Demonstrations
  • Hands-on labs
  • Live sessions

The mix of learning activities varies across modules.

Periodic live sessions are approximately 2 hours, while the remaining learner effort is expected to be devoted to self-paced learning, demonstrations, and practical lab work.

32. IMPORTANT BOT FAQ RESPONSES

Is the programme fully online?

Yes. The programme is delivered entirely online.

 

Is it suitable for working professionals?

Yes. The programme is specifically designed for working professionals and follows a flexible flipped-classroom model.

 

What is the weekly time commitment?

Learners are expected to spend approximately 9–10 hours per week, including self-paced learning, labs, demonstrations, and live sessions.

 

Do I need prior work experience?

Yes. Applicants must have a minimum of 2 years of relevant work experience in software development, technology, data, analytics, cloud, AI/ML, IT, or allied domains.

 

Is Python mandatory?

Applicants are expected to have foundational proficiency in Python Programming.

Applicants who require additional preparation may opt for the Bridge Programme.

 

What if I do not have the required foundational proficiency?

Applicants may enrol in the optional 8-week Bridge Programme covering Python Programming, AWS Fundamentals, and Mathematics Essentials for AI before progressing to the Professional Certificate programme.

 

Will I work on a capstone project?

Yes. Learners complete an industry-grade capstone project individually under the guidance of an industry professional.

 

Are cloud costs included in the programme fee?

No. Certain advanced modules may require learners to use AWS cloud services or third-party AI services. Any such usage costs are borne by the learner and are not included in the programme fee.

 

Can the curriculum or tools change?

Yes. The curriculum, technologies, tools, learning components, and sequencing are indicative and may be revised by BITS Pilani based on academic review, evolving technologies, and industry requirements.
