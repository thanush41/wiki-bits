PROFESSIONAL CERTIFICATE IN AI ENGINEERING & MLOps – TOOLS, TECHNOLOGIES AND PLATFORMS

Learners gain practical, hands-on experience with a range of leading AI Engineering and MLOps technologies used to build, deploy, scale, and manage production-grade AI systems. The programme includes hands-on exposure to some of the leading AWS equivalents of open-source AI and MLOps technologies such as MLflow, vLLM, and ONNX, enabling learners to develop the skills required to deploy, scale, and manage AI workloads in enterprise production environments.

Please note that the tools and technologies covered in the programme may be updated from time to time based on the Institute's assessment of evolving technologies and what is considered most appropriate for learners.

Development & Programming Frameworks

PyTorch, TensorFlow, and Streamlit for model development, training, and interactive visualization.

MLOps & Pipeline Orchestration Tools

Apache Airflow, Apache Beam, Kubeflow, Flyte, MLflow, Ray, and KubeRay for workflow automation, distributed computing, and experiment tracking.

Containerization & Deployment Platforms

Docker, Kubernetes, Serverless, Truss, NVIDIA Triton, vLLM, TensorRT, ONNX, AWS SageMaker, and Hugging Face Inference for scalable deployment and model serving.

AI-Specific Frameworks & Tools

LangChain, LlamaIndex, AutoGen, and ParlAI for building intelligent retrieval systems, conversational agents, and LLM-driven applications.

Data Engineering & Management Tools

Apache Kafka, Apache Flink, ELK Stack, Apache Spark, Label Studio, DVC, and Great Expectations for real-time data processing, versioning, and validation.

Monitoring, Observability & Telemetry

Prometheus, Grafana, Splunk, OpenTelemetry, TensorBoard, and Weights & Biases (W&B) for system monitoring, performance tracking, and model observability.

Optimization & Performance Tools

Flash Attention, Speculative Decoding, KV Caching, Quantization, Model Pruning, Optuna, and Hyperopt for improving model efficiency and scalability.

Project Management & Collaboration Tools

ClickUp, GitOps, Infrastructure-as-Code, and CI/CD for structured collaboration, automated workflows, and reproducible engineering.

Benchmarks & Evaluation Metrics

BLEU, Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1 Score, and ML Test Score for objective model evaluation and performance benchmarking.
