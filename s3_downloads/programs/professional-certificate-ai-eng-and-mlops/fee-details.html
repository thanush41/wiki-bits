Optional Bridge Programme

Applicants who wish to strengthen their foundational proficiency before commencing the Professional Certificate programme may opt for the 8-week Bridge Programme.

Bridge Programme Fee

₹25,000 + GST

The Bridge Programme covers foundational concepts in:

  • Python Programming
  • AWS Fundamentals
  • Mathematics Essentials for AI

Learners engage with self-paced learning during the week and attend live instructor-led sessions, scheduled mostly on weekends. Successful completion of the Bridge Programme is subject to completion of the prescribed learning content, required quizzes, and a minimum of 75% attendance in the live sessions.

Upon successful completion, learners will submit a self-assessment declaration confirming their readiness to pursue the Professional Certificate in AI Engineering & MLOps. They may then be considered for admission to the next available batch, subject to batch availability and the Institute's admission policies.

What's Included in the Programme Fee

The Professional Certificate programme fee includes:

  • Access to all self-paced learning modules
  • Live instructor-led sessions
  • Hands-on labs and practical learning exercises (where applicable)
  • Learning resources and programme materials
  • Access to Teaching Assistants and discussion forums
  • Capstone project guidance from industry professionals
  • Assessments and programme completion requirements

Some labs may use shared, open-source, or free-tier environments. However, certain advanced modules may require learners to use AWS cloud services, including GPU compute, storage, virtual machines, and related cloud resources, as well as tokens from providers such as OpenAI, Gemini, or Anthropic. Any AWS usage costs incurred for such activities will be funded by the learner and are not included in the programme fee.
