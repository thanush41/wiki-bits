LEARNING METHODOLOGY

The program follows a flipped-classroom model, combining flexible self-paced learning with structured live sessions.

Learn through a Flipped Classroom Methodology

The program follows a flipped-classroom methodology, where learners first build conceptual understanding through self-paced learning material. Wherever applicable, they complete practice exercises and labs to apply these concepts before attending periodic live sessions scheduled mostly on weekends.

The live sessions are designed for instructor interaction, doubt resolution, deeper discussion, and augmented learning.

Weekly Learning Rhythm

Learner effort is expected to be typically 9–10 hours per week, engaging with the program through self-paced learning material, demonstrations, laboratory practice, and live sessions.

The mix of learning components may vary from week to week depending on the requirements of each module.

Periodic live sessions are approximately 2 hours in duration, while the remaining learner effort is expected to be devoted to self-paced study, demonstrations, and practical lab work.

Weekly Live Instructor Interactions

Learners attend weekly live sessions of approximately two hours, scheduled mostly on weekends.

These sessions are led by subject matter experts, many of whom are experienced industry practitioners.

The sessions bridge conceptual understanding with practical implementation, helping learners develop confidence while progressing through the programme.

Support through Teaching Assistants

Learning support extends beyond the live sessions through dedicated Teaching Assistants and discussion forums.

Learners may use these forums to seek conceptual clarification, receive guidance on practical exercises, and obtain support while completing labs and assignments wherever applicable.

ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA

Applicants seeking admission to the Professional Certificate in AI Engineering and MLOps must meet the following eligibility requirements.

Foundational Proficiency

Applicants are expected to possess foundational proficiency in:

  • Python Programming
  • AWS Fundamentals
  • Mathematics Essentials for AI

Applicants should be familiar with the topics outlined in the Foundational Proficiency Topics document. (Link to be added.)

Work Experience

Applicants must possess a minimum of two years of relevant professional work experience in one or more of the following domains:

  • Software Development
  • Information Technology
  • Data Engineering
  • Data Analytics
  • Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine Learning
  • Cloud Computing
  • Other allied technology domains

Educational Qualification

Applicants must possess a Bachelor's degree from a recognised university, with Mathematics as part of the curriculum.

Self-Declaration

Applicants must submit a self-declaration confirming that they possess foundational proficiency in Python Programming, AWS Fundamentals, and Mathematics Essentials for AI.

Bridge Pathway

Applicants who do not currently satisfy the foundational proficiency requirements may choose to enrol in the 8-week Bridge Programme, covering:

  • Python Programming
  • AWS Fundamentals
  • Mathematics Essentials for AI

Upon successful completion of the Bridge Programme, learners may be considered for admission to the next available batch of the Professional Certificate in AI Engineering and MLOps, subject to batch availability and the Institute's admission policies.

APPLICATION PROCESS
