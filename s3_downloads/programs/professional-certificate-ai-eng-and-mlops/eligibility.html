Professional Certificate in AI Engineering & MLOps – Eligibility

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants seeking admission to the Professional Certificate in AI Engineering & MLOps must meet the following eligibility requirements.

Educational Qualification

Applicants must possess a Bachelor's degree from a recognised university, with Mathematics as part of the curriculum.

Work Experience

Applicants must have a minimum of 2 years of relevant work experience in one or more of the following domains:

  • Software Development
  • Information Technology (IT)
  • Data Engineering
  • Data Analytics
  • Artificial Intelligence (AI)
  • Machine Learning (ML)
  • Cloud Computing
  • Other allied technology domains

Foundational Proficiency Requirements

Applicants are expected to possess foundational proficiency in the following areas:

  • Python Programming
  • AWS Fundamentals
  • Mathematics Essentials for AI

Applicants should be familiar with the topics outlined in the Foundational Proficiency Topics document.

Self-Declaration of Foundational Proficiency

As part of the application process, applicants must submit a self-declaration confirming that they possess foundational proficiency in:

  • Python Programming
  • AWS Fundamentals
  • Mathematics Essentials for AI

Bridge Programme (Optional)

Applicants who do not currently meet the foundational proficiency requirements may choose to enrol in the 8-week Bridge Programme.

The Bridge Programme is designed to help learners strengthen their foundations in:

  • Python Programming
  • AWS Fundamentals
  • Mathematics Essentials for AI

Bridge Programme Details

Duration: 8 Weeks

Fee: ₹25,000 + GST

Learning Format:

  • Self-paced learning material
  • Live instructor-led classes, scheduled mostly on weekends

Successful Completion Requirements

To successfully complete the Bridge Programme, learners must:

  • Complete all prescribed self-paced learning content.
  • Attempt all required quizzes.
  • Maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in the live classes.

Upon successful completion of the Bridge Programme, learners will be required to submit a self-assessment declaration confirming that they are adequately prepared to pursue the Professional Certificate in AI Engineering & MLOps.

Following this, learners may be considered for admission to the next available batch of the programme, subject to batch availability and the Institute's admission policies.

Important Note

Meeting the published eligibility requirements or completing the Bridge Programme does not guarantee admission.

All applications are reviewed by the BITS Pilani Digital Admissions Team, and admission decisions are made based on the Institute's admission policies and evaluation process.
