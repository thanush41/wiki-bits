Bachelor of Science in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence - Eligibility

 

Indian Applicants

Educational background

You must have completed Class 12 in any stream, or hold a government recognized technical diploma (10+3). In-case you are still waiting for your 12th standard results, secure your seat(after successfully meeting all eligibility criteria ahead of the batch start date) by completing the application form and paying the admission fee.

Maths/Statistics requirement

You need at least 60% marks in Maths/Statistics in Class 12 or in your Technical Diploma. 

OR Clear an online Math Eligibility Test (up to 2 attempts). 

OR Submit proof of enrollment in a STEM undergraduate degree. 

OR Show you've qualified for the JEE Advanced.

Didn’t meet the Math criteria? No worries! You can still qualify through:

- Enroll in a preparatory bridge course and clear it. 

Duration: 4 months

Fees: INR 8,250

- On clearing this course, you will be eligible to enrol in the program for the next admission cycle

English Proficiency 

Since the program is taught in English, you’ll need to be comfortable with the language.

Age Requirement

There’s no age limit – we believe, it’s never too early or too late to start learning.

Work Experience

Not required. This program is designed for freshers and professionals alike.

International Applicants

Educational background

You must have completed high school examination (or its equivalent) under your national board and you need to provide a University Grants Commission equivalence certificate/UGC equivalence certificate.

Maths/Statistics requirement

You need a minimum of 60% marks in Maths/Statistics in your Class 12 (or equivalent) examination.

OR

Take BITS Pilani’s preparatory Math course and pass it.

OR

Show proof of enrolment in a STEM undergraduate program.

OR

Submit proof of a valid SAT score (920+) or JEE Advanced qualification.

Didn’t meet the Math criteria?

No problem! There are other ways:

- Enrol in a preparatory bridge course and clear it.

Duration: 4 months | Fees: USD 100

- On clearing this course, you will be eligible to enrol in the program for the next admission cycle.

English Proficiency

Since the program is taught in English, you’ll need to be comfortable with the language.

Age Requirement

There’s no age limit – we believe, it’s never too early or too late to start learning.

Work Experience

Not required. This program is designed for freshers and professionals alike.
