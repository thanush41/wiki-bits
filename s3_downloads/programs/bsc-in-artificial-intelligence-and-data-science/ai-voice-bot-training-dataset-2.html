“Can I use MacBook Air?”  

“What laptop should I buy?”  

“Is Mac compatible?”  

Response: 

You do not need a very high-end system to get started with the programme. 

A standard laptop with an Intel i5 / Ryzen 5 (or equivalent) processor and at least 8–16 GB RAM is sufficient for most coursework  

Both Windows and macOS devices (including MacBook Air) are compatible

If you plan to work on advanced projects or large datasets, a higher configuration may provide a smoother experience, but it is not mandatory. 

If you need help choosing a specific device or configuration, our team can guide you based on your use case.

Additional Post-Enrollment FAQs & Standard Responses

Payment & Fee Related

QUESTION 19: I have paid the fee, but it is not showing in my portal.

Suggested Response

Payment reconciliation may take approximately 24–48 working hours from the time of successful payment.

During this period, payment status, enrollment status, and receipts may not immediately reflect in the Application Centre.

If more than 48 working hours have passed since payment, please write to admissions@bitspilani-digital.edu.in with:

  • Application Number
  • Payment Transaction ID
  • Payment Screenshot

The Admissions Team will review and assist you.

QUESTION 20:

I have paid the fee but am still receiving payment reminders.

Suggested Response

We regret the confusion.

Automated reminders may continue until the payment reconciliation process is completed.

If you have already paid, no further action is required from your side at this stage.

If the payment status remains unchanged after 48 working hours, please write to admissions@bitspilani-digital.edu.in.

QUESTION 21:

I have not received my fee receipt.

Suggested Response

Fee receipts become available after payment reconciliation is completed.

For newly admitted learners, receipts may take approximately 3–5 working days to become available.

If your receipt is still not visible after this period, please write to admissions@bitspilani-digital.edu.in with your Application Number and payment details.
