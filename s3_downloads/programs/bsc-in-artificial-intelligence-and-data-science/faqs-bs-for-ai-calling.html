1. What is BITS Pilani Digital? 

BITS Pilani Digital is the digital learning division of BITS Pilani, one of India’s most prestigious and future-focused institutions. It reimagines higher education for a new generation of learners — those who seek world-class academic excellence without being bound by geography, full-time campus commitments, or rigid entry barriers.  Backed by the academic legacy of BITS Pilani and driven by the possibilities of digital innovation, BITS Pilani Digital is here to make exceptional education more accessible, more employable, and more impactful. 

 

2. What is Bachelor of Science in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence ? 

The Bachelor of Science in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence from BITS Pilani Digital is a cutting-edge undergraduate degree designed to equip students with deep technical skills and real-world problem-solving abilities in two of the most in-demand domains today — Data Science and AI. Built on a strong foundation in mathematics, programming, and analytics, the programme blends academic rigour with hands-on project work, critical thinking, and industry-relevant applications. Students can also choose from multiple exit options: a Certificate after 1 year, Diploma after 2 years, B.Sc. after 3 years, or continue to earn the full 4-year B.S. Degree. Whether you're aiming for a career in tech, research, or innovation, this programme prepares you to thrive in a data-driven world — with the flexibility, support, and credibility of a BITS Pilani education

6. What is the difference here between the 3 Years B.Sc. degree in Data Science & AI and the 4 Years B.S Degree in Data Science & AI offered by BITS Pilani? Which one should I choose in my application? 

The 3-Year B.Sc. in Data Science & AI is also a complete undergraduate degree programme designed for students who wish to earn a bachelor’s qualification in three years. The 4-Year B.S. Degree includes all the learning of the B.Sc. in the first three years and then builds further with a fourth year of specialised courses, advanced projects, and deeper exposure to emerging areas in Data Science and AI. 

Both programmes have the same admission eligibility criteria, and students can choose based on their long-term goals: 

     i) If you wish to pursue a 3-year undergraduate degree, you can enrol in the B.Sc. 

     ii) If you’re aiming for a 4-year degree particularly if you're considering Master’s admissions abroad or institutes, where a 4-year undergraduate degree is often required, or you want to gain greater depth and specialisation — the B.S. is the better fit. 

What adds to the flexibility is that BITS Pilani Digital allows you to decide or change your path as you go: 

     i) Students enrolled in the B.S. programme can exit after three years with a B.Sc. degree, if they choose. 

    ii) Students enrolled in the B.Sc. programme can upgrade to the B.S. in their third year and continue into the fourth year. 

This structure gives you the freedom to start where you’re comfortable, and based on your evolving plans, exit or extend at the right time — without compromising on quality or recognition. 

 

7. What is the mode of learning for this degree? 

The mode of learning is online, with classes mostly scheduled on Fridays, Saturdays, and Sundays. The curriculum is thoughtfully designed to balance flexibility, structure, and academic excellence. All teaching, mentoring, lab work, and ongoing assessments are conducted through BITS Pilani’s advanced Digital Learning Platform, enabling you to learn from anywhere, at your own pace. 

While the entire learning experience is delivered online, students residing in India are required to appear for end-of-trimester exams at officially designated BITS Pilani examination centres across major cities. This ensures the flexibility of digital learning while upholding the rigour and credibility of a BITS Pilani academic evaluation. 

 

8. Is the Degree equivalent to those awarded in the conventional mode? 

As per Clause 22 of the UGC (ODL & Online Programmes) Regulations, 2020, degrees awarded by recognized institutions through online mode are considered equivalent to degrees offered through the conventional mode.

12. What if I am unable to complete the full degree programme? Will I still get a recognised qualification? 

Yes, absolutely. The programme is designed with multiple exit options to ensure that your academic progress is recognised meaningfully — even if you don’t complete the full 4 year degree. 

     i) After completing the credit/unit requirements of 1 year, you can exit with a Certificate in Data Science & AI. 

     ii) After completing the credit/unit requirements of 2 years, you can exit with a Diploma in Data Science & AI. 

     iii) After completing the credit requirements of 3 years, you can exit with a B.Sc. Degree in Data Science & AI. 

     iv) After completing all 4 years, you earn the full B.S. Degree in Data Science & AI. 

Each credential is a valuable milestone, equipping you with practical, in-demand skills and enhancing your employability at every stage — whether you choose to enter the workforce, pursue internships, or continue your education 

 

13. Can I take fewer than the prescribed no of courses in a trimester? 

Yes, you can. The programme is designed to offer flexibility that fits your pace and priorities. While there is a recommended number of courses per trimester to help you complete the degree on time, you are free to take fewer courses in any given trimester if you need to manage other academic, personal, or professional commitments. This allows you to customise your learning journey based on your capacity and goals. Whether you want to slow down during a particularly busy period or spread out your workload more comfortably, the structure of the programme supports you in doing so without any academic penalty 

 

14. Can I take a break for a trimester or more? 

Yes, absolutely. The programme offers the flexibility to take a break for one or more trimesters if you need to pause your studies due to personal, academic, or professional reasons. This ensures that your progress is not lost, and you can return when you're ready. 
That said, here are a few important things to note: 

     i) A break can only be taken before the start of a trimester. Once a trimester begins and you've accessed classes or learning materials, a break is no longer permitted for that term. 

     ii) When you resume the programme after a break, you will be required to pay a continuity fee for each trimester of break, in addition to the regular trimester fee for the trimester you register for. 

This structure allows you to manage your learning journey on your own terms, while ensuring a smooth and supported return to the programme whenever you're ready to continue. 

 

15. What is the maximum time allowed to complete the programme? 

You are allowed a maximum of 8 years to complete the full 4-year B.S. Degree in Data Science & AI. This generous timeframe is designed to give you the flexibility to balance your studies with other academic, professional, or personal commitments. 
Within this 8-year window, you may choose to take fewer courses in a trimester, take approved breaks between trimesters, or adjust your pace — as long as you meet all academic requirements and credit obligations within the permitted duration. 

If you opt to exit earlier with an interim credential, you have up to double the normal duration to complete each stage: 

     i) The 1-year Certificate can be completed in up to 2 years 

     ii) The 2-year Diploma can be completed in up to 4 years 

     iii) The 3-year B.Sc. Degree can be completed in up to 6 years 

There are no additional fee implications for progressing at a slower pace. The programme follows a trimester-based fee model — meaning the trimester fee remains the same regardless of the number of courses you choose to register for in that term. The only additional charge is a continuity fee, which applies only if you take a full break for one or more trimesters. This fee is paid at the time of resuming the programme. 

This flexible structure ensures that your learning stays aligned with your life, while still allowing you to earn a prestigious BITS Pilani credential — on your own terms and timeline. 

 

16. What is the Eligibility Criteria? 

To be eligible for admission, you must meet the following requirements: 

Educational Background: 
You should have completed Class 12 or equivalent in any stream, or hold a government-recognised Post-Class 10 Technical Diploma. 

Maths/Statistics Requirement: 
You must have scored at least 60% marks in Mathematics or Statistics in your Class 12 exams or in your Technical Diploma. 

Pathway 1: Direct Eligibility 
 You must have secured a minimum of 60% marks in Mathematics or Statistics in your Class 12 examination or Technical Diploma. 

Pathway 2: Eligibility via Math Bridge Course 
 If you did not study Mathematics/Statistics in Class 12 or scored below 60%, you may instead qualify by successfully completing the Math Bridge Course offered by BITS Pilani Digital. 

     i) Duration: 4 months 

     ii) Fee: ₹8,250 

     iii) Upon successful completion, you become eligible to enrol in the degree program in the next admission cycle.

17. I did not study Maths in Class 12 , Can I apply? 

For those who do not meet the Maths Criteria: 
No worries! You still have multiple options as given below to qualify: 

     i) Take and successfully complete BITS Pilani’s preparatory Math course 

     ii) Clear an online Math Eligibility Test (up to 2 attempts) 

     iii) Submit proof of current enrolment in a STEM undergraduate degree 

     iv) Show proof of having qualified JEE Advanced 

While you can choose any option above, however the most recommended would be to enrol in a Preparatory Mathematics course offered by BITS Pilani, which runs for approximately 4 months. Upon successfully clearing the final exam for this course, you will become eligible for admission into the degree programme. The enrolment fee for this course is INR 8,250, payable at the time of registration. 

  

This multi-pathway approach ensures that students from diverse backgrounds still have a fair opportunity to join the programme, while maintaining the academic standards needed for success in Data Science & AI.

19. How to apply for this programme? 

Applying is easy and takes just a few steps. Here’s the simple 3-step process: 

     i) Fill the Online Application Form (https://applications.bitspilani-digital.edu.in/) 
     Provide primarily details about your educational background and personal information in the form. 

     ii) Pay the Application Fee 
     Submit a non-refundable application fee of INR 1,500 through the secure online payment gateway. 

     iii) Upload Supporting Documents 
     You’ll be asked to upload relevant documents such as your government-issued ID, Class 12 marksheet, diploma certificate (if applicable), and any other documents needed to verify your eligibility. 

Once submitted, your application will be reviewed by the admissions team, and you’ll be informed about the next steps. The process is designed to be quick, smooth, and entirely online — so you can apply from wherever you are. 

 

20. How will I know that I am selected? 

Once you submit your application, it will be evaluated by the Admissions Committee, which will verify your educational qualifications and review your supporting documents for completeness. 

If any documents are missing or need clarification, the Admissions Cell will contact you to help resolve the issue. 

If you haven’t met the Mathematics eligibility requirement, the Admissions Cell will reach out to you with details of two options: 

     i) Enrol in the paid Preparatory Mathematics Course, or 

     ii) Take the Online Mathematics Eligibility Test (up to two attempts) 

Based on your choice, they will assist you in either enrolling for the course or registering for the test. 
If your application meets all eligibility criteria, you will receive an official Offer Letter along with detailed instructions to accept the offer and complete your admission by paying the one-time admission fee and the first trimester fee. 
The process is designed to be smooth, transparent, and supportive — ensuring you're informed and guided at every step. 

 

21. What is the learning methodology? 

The programme follows a unique trimester-based learning model that combines academic rigour with maximum flexibility. Each week is carefully structured to help you grasp concepts deeply while giving you the freedom to learn at your own pace — making it ideal for both full-time learners and those balancing other commitments. 

Here’s how your weekly learning journey is designed: 

     i) Start with Self-Paced Learning 
At the beginning of each week, you’ll access high-quality video lectures and engaging digital materials developed and delivered by BITS Pilani faculty, through our advanced Digital Learning Platform. 

     ii) Get On-the-Go Support 
If you have questions, you can post them anytime to our dedicated academic helpdesk. Our experienced teaching assistants respond promptly to ensure your learning stays on track. 

     iii) Engage in Live Faculty Sessions 
At the end of each week, you’ll attend live, discussion-rich online classes, conducted mostly on Fridays, Saturdays, or Sundays. These sessions allow for real-time, two-way interaction with faculty, helping you strengthen your understanding and clarify any doubts. 

     iv) Practice with Online Cloud Labs 
For lab-oriented courses, you’ll gain hands-on experience using virtual or remote labs, accessible securely from anywhere. These cloud labs simulate real-world environments, ensuring practical, application-focused learning. 

     v) All teaching, mentoring, labs, and assessments (except end of trimester exams for students in India) are conducted entirely online. 

     vi) End of Trimester Exams: The only in-person requirement applicable for residents of India is for end of trimester examinations, which are conducted at officially designated physical exam centres identified by BITS Pilani across various cities in India. 

This flexible, digital-first methodology ensures you receive a high-quality, BITS Pilani education from anywhere, while maintaining academic rigour through structured evaluation at the end of each trimester.
