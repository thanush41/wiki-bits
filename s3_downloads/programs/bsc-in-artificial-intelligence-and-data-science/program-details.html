Multiple Exit Points

Our programs are designed with exceptional flexibility, offering multiple exit options to suit your evolving goals and life circumstances. Whether you're looking to upskill, pause, or transition at any point, you can do so without compromising on quality or progress—empowering you to learn at your own pace and define success on your own terms.

a) Certificate in Data Science - If you choose to exit after completing the first 3 trimesters (normally after one year), you’ll earn a Certificate in Data Science - a strong first credential that opens doors to exciting foundational roles.

b) Diploma in Data Science - Complete 6 trimesters (normally after 2 years), and you’ll receive a Diploma in Data Science - a powerful proof of your expertise and readiness to take on more advanced, applied roles in the industry.

c) BSc - 3 years (Data Science & AI) - Stay the full 9 trimesters (normally after 3 years), and you’ll graduate with a BSc degree in Data Science & AI from BITS Pilani - a mark of excellence and a passport to leadership in an AI-powered world.

B.S. in Data Science & AI – Mathematics Bridge Programme

Knowledge Base for AI Chatbot, Voice Bot & Admissions Counselors

Overview

The Mathematics Bridge Programme is a pathway programme available for certain applicants to the B.S. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence programme.

The Bridge Programme is intended for applicants who do not currently satisfy the mathematics eligibility requirement for direct admission into the B.S. programme but may be eligible to enter through the approved bridge pathway.

Unlike the M.Sc. Preparedness Programmes, the Mathematics Bridge Programme is an academic pathway to the B.S. programme.

Why is the Mathematics Bridge Programme Offered?

The B.S. in Data Science & AI programme requires mathematical readiness because learners will study subjects such as:

  • Mathematics
  • Statistics
  • Data Science
  • Machine Learning
  • Artificial Intelligence

Some applicants may not meet the standard mathematics eligibility requirements for direct admission.

The Mathematics Bridge Programme provides an alternate pathway that enables eligible learners to strengthen their mathematical foundations before entering the B.S. programme.

Who May Be Eligible for the Mathematics Bridge Programme?

The Mathematics Bridge Programme may be relevant for applicants who:

  • Did not study Mathematics in Class 12, or
  • Do not meet the mathematics eligibility requirement for direct admission, or
  • Are advised by the Admissions Team to consider the bridge pathway

Final eligibility is determined through the admissions review process.

Is the Mathematics Bridge Programme a Pathway to the B.S. Programme?

Yes.

The Mathematics Bridge Programme is an approved pathway into the B.S. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence programme for eligible applicants.

Applicants who successfully complete the Mathematics Bridge Programme may become eligible to progress into the B.S. programme, subject to applicable academic requirements and University policies.

Can I Join the B.S. Programme Directly Instead of the Bridge Programme?

If you already satisfy the published mathematics eligibility requirements for the B.S. programme, you may be considered for direct admission.

The Bridge Programme is intended only for applicants who require the bridge pathway to satisfy the mathematics requirement.

What Does the Mathematics Bridge Programme Cover?

The programme is designed to strengthen foundational mathematical concepts required for success in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence studies.

Specific topics, schedules, and academic requirements are communicated to enrolled learners.

Is the Mathematics Bridge Programme Online?

Yes.

The programme is delivered through the BITS Pilani Digital learning ecosystem.

Access details, schedules, and onboarding instructions are shared with enrolled learners.

How Long is the Mathematics Bridge Programme?

The Bridge Programme is conducted before commencement of the B.S. programme.

Specific duration, schedules, and academic calendar details are communicated separately to eligible learners.

What Happens After Completing the Bridge Programme?

Learners who successfully complete the Mathematics Bridge Programme and satisfy the applicable academic requirements may proceed into the B.S. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence programme in accordance with University policies.

The Admissions Team will communicate the next steps and commencement timeline.

Can the Mathematics Bridge Programme and B.S. Programme Run Together?

No.

The Mathematics Bridge Programme must be completed before beginning the B.S. programme.

The Bridge Programme and the first trimester of the B.S. programme do not run in parallel.

Is the Bridge Programme Optional?

For applicants who require the bridge pathway to satisfy the mathematics eligibility requirement, the Mathematics Bridge Programme forms part of the admission pathway.

Applicants who already satisfy the mathematics eligibility criteria do not need to enroll in the Bridge Programme.

Frequently Asked Questions

I did not study Mathematics in Class 12. Can I still apply?

Yes.

You may submit an application for review. Depending on your profile and eligibility, you may be considered for the Mathematics Bridge Programme pathway.

Does the Bridge Programme guarantee admission?

Applicants must satisfy all applicable academic and programme requirements.

Progression to the B.S. programme is governed by University policies and academic requirements applicable to the Bridge Programme.

Is the Mathematics Bridge Programme the same as the M.Sc. Preparedness Programme?

No.

The Mathematics Bridge Programme for the B.S. programme is an admission pathway for eligible applicants.

The Mathematics and Programming Preparedness Programmes offered in connection with the M.Sc. programme are optional refresher programmes and are not admission pathways.

Can I skip the Bridge Programme?

Applicants who meet the direct admission eligibility criteria for the B.S. programme may not require the Bridge Programme.

Applicants who require the bridge pathway must follow the pathway communicated by the Admissions Team.
