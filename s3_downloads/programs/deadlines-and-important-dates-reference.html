1.1 B.S. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence

 

B.S. Mathematics Eligibility Pathways – Important Guidance

Applicants who meet the prescribed Mathematics eligibility criteria and have received admission may commence the B.S. programme directly as per the applicable academic schedule.

Mathematics Qualifier Test (MQT): The MQT is an alternate eligibility pathway for applicants who are directed to take the test following evaluation of their B.S. application. The 5 September 2026 MQT is the final MQT opportunity for consideration for direct B.S. Trimester 1 admission for the September 2026 intake.

Applicants who do not meet the prescribed Mathematics eligibility criteria may, following application evaluation and the Admissions Committee decision, receive a Provisional Offer of Admission through a pathway beginning with the Foundation Course in Mathematics.

  • The Foundation Course in Mathematics is delivered over one trimester.
  • It forms part of the learner’s B.S. admission pathway and is not a standalone programme.
  • Classes for the Foundation Course in Mathematics commence on 25 September 2026.
  • Successful completion of the Foundation Course in Mathematics and clearance of the prescribed assessment are mandatory before the learner can commence the subsequent courses in the B.S. programme structure.

1.2 M.Sc. in Data Science & Artificial Intelligence

 

M.Sc. Mathematics & Programming Preparedness Programmes – Important Guidance

The Mathematics and Programming Preparedness Programmes are optional academic-support programmes. They are offered to learners who selected the applicable Preparedness Programme(s) while submitting their M.Sc. application.

For the September 2026 cohort, learners who have selected the Mathematics and/or Programming Preparedness Programme are informed to undertake the selected Preparedness Programme(s) in parallel with M.Sc. Trimester 1.

  • The learner begins the M.Sc. programme with the September 2026 cohort.
  • M.Sc. Orientation is on 11 September 2026.
  • The selected Mathematics and/or Programming Preparedness Programme is undertaken alongside M.Sc. Trimester 1.
  • Preparedness Programmes are optional and do not replace, waive or substitute the prescribed M.Sc. admission eligibility criteria.
  • Applicable Preparedness Programme and Trimester 1 fees are governed by the learner’s Offer Letter and current University fee policy.

If a learner no longer wishes to pursue the optional Preparedness Programme(s)

A learner who selected Mathematics and/or Programming Preparedness while submitting the application but subsequently decides not to undertake the optional programme(s) may request the Admissions Team to update the M.Sc. offer without the selected Preparedness Programme(s).

The learner should email admissions@bitspilani-digital.edu.in and mention:

  • Full Name
  • Application Number
  • Preparedness Programme(s) they wish to opt out of

The application/offer will then be reviewed and, where applicable, an updated M.Sc. Offer Letter without the selected Preparedness Programme(s) will be released.

4. Important Onboarding & Admissions Guidance

  • Applicants who have already received an Offer Letter must follow the payment/admission deadline specified in their Offer Letter or subsequent official communication.
  • Payments should be made only through the authorised payment option available through the Application Portal/Offer Letter.
  • Payment reconciliation and corresponding portal status updates may take approximately 24–48 hours.
  • Applicants who have already completed an applicable payment should not make a duplicate payment merely because an automated reminder is received.
  • Orientation links and joining instructions are shared with eligible/admitted learners on their registered email address closer to the respective Orientation.
  • LMS/student credentials and onboarding access are shared as part of the onboarding process and are not necessarily generated immediately after fee payment.
  • Academic calendars, detailed class schedules, examination schedules, course handouts and learner handbooks are communicated separately through the appropriate academic/onboarding channels.
  • All admissions remain subject to fulfilment of the applicable eligibility criteria, document verification, current Offer Letter terms, BITS Pilani Education Policy and applicable University regulations.
  • Guidance provided by counsellors or the AI Assistant should not be represented as a final eligibility or admission decision where formal application review is required.
  • Where an applicant’s programme pathway, offer or eligibility requires reconsideration, the case should be referred for the applicable Admissions Committee review, and the official outcome should be communicated through the registered email/Application Portal.

Orientation – What to Expect and How to Join

The Orientation session is designed to familiarise learners with the programme structure, academic calendar, class schedule, learning model, LMS and digital learning environment, course registration process, assessment and examination framework, applicable academic policies, learner support channels, and the key academic and administrative steps to be completed after enrolment. The session will also explain how learners can access their courses, academic resources and subsequent programme communications. Orientation will be conducted online through Zoom. Learners should have access to a Zoom account; a free Zoom account is sufficient and no paid subscription is required. No separate BITS-specific Zoom login is required to access the Orientation; learners should join through the official link shared on their registered email address closer to the scheduled session. Learners are advised to check their Inbox as well as Spam/Junk folders, ensure Zoom is installed or accessible on their device, and join a few minutes before the scheduled start time. LMS/student credentials, where applicable, may be shared separately as part of the onboarding process and should not be assumed to be available immediately after fee payment.

DEB ID

  • For learners residing in India and enrolled in applicable degree programmes, the UGC-DEB ID requirement must be completed within the prescribed regulatory timeline.
  • The DEB ID must be submitted on the Application Portal within 30 days from the Batch Commencement Date, as applicable.
  • For learners residing outside India, the DEB ID requirement is not applicable.

International Qualification / Equivalence Requirement

Where an international learner’s qualifying academic credential requires equivalence, the applicable UGC/AIU equivalence certificate must be submitted within 30 days from the Batch Commencement Date, in accordance with the applicable regulatory requirements.

If the required equivalence certificate is not submitted within the prescribed 30-day timeline, the learner will not be permitted to appear for the end-of-trimester examination until the requirement is fulfilled. Continued registration, academic progression, evaluation and certification will remain subject to completion of the required equivalence documentation in accordance with University and regulatory requirements.

5. Key Knowledge-Base Guardrails

B.S. Foundation Course in Mathematics

Required pathway wording: Foundation Course in Mathematics → Successful completion and clearance of prescribed assessment → Progression to subsequent B.S. courses.

  • Do not state that payment of the B.S. Trimester 1 fee allows the learner to begin subsequent B.S. courses before successful completion of the Foundation Course.
  • Do not describe the Foundation Course in Mathematics as a standalone programme.
  • Use “Foundation Course in Mathematics” in current applicant-facing communication. Avoid “Maths Bridge” or “Bridge Programme” for the B.S. pathway except when acknowledging terminology used by a learner in an existing communication.

M.Sc. Mathematics & Programming Preparedness Programmes

Standard September 2026 pathway wording: M.Sc. Trimester 1 + selected Mathematics/Programming Preparedness Programme(s) in parallel.

  • Preparedness Programmes are optional and apply to learners who selected them while submitting their M.Sc. application.
  • Do not confuse the M.Sc. Preparedness model with the B.S. Foundation Course model.
  • Preparedness Programmes do not waive or substitute the prescribed M.Sc. admission eligibility criteria.
  • If a learner wishes to opt out, direct them to admissions@bitspilani-digital.edu.in for review and an updated Offer Letter, where applicable.
  • Do not advise the learner to manually deduct the Preparedness fee from an existing Offer Letter.

Eligibility & Admission Decisions

  • The AI Assistant may explain published eligibility criteria but should not independently provide a final eligibility determination where formal review of the submitted application and academic documents is required.
  • Admissions Counsellors and the AI Assistant provide guidance based on available information. Final eligibility/admission decisions are made through the applicable institutional review process.

Offer Letter & Fee Guidance

The learner’s current Offer Letter/Application Portal is the authoritative reference for applicable admission fees and payment requirements.

  • Do not instruct a learner to manually deduct a fee from an Offer Letter.
  • Do not instruct a learner to make a partial payment not supported by the portal.
  • Do not instruct a learner to use an unofficial payment link.
  • Do not instruct a learner to make a second payment while an earlier authorised payment is awaiting reconciliation.
  • Where an Offer Letter needs modification, the learner should wait for the updated Offer Letter/payment instruction before proceeding, unless Admissions has specifically advised otherwise.
