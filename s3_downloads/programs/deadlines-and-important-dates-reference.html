3. Important Guidance

  • Admissions are reviewed on a rolling basis.
  • Applications are evaluated as they are received. Eligible candidates may receive admission offers before the final application deadline.
  • As programmes have limited seats, admissions may close earlier if all available seats are filled.
  • Applicants are encouraged to complete their applications and admission formalities at the earliest.
  • Orientation links, LMS access, and onboarding communication are shared on the registered email address before programme commencement, post Orientation session.
  • Academic calendars, class schedules, examination schedules, and learner handbooks are shared separately after admission confirmation.
  • All admissions remain subject to fulfilment of the prescribed eligibility criteria, document verification, and approval by the Admissions Committee.
