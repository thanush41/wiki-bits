Application Start Questions

Q1. I started my application but could not complete it. Can I continue from where I left?

Updated Answer

Yes.

Your application is automatically saved as you progress.

You may resume your application at any time before the application deadline by logging in through the official Application Portal using your registered email address:

https://applications.bitspilani-digital.edu.in/

If you are unable to access your application or experience any technical issue, please email the Admissions Team with a screenshot of the error so that the issue can be investigated.

 

Q2. I forgot my application login password.

Updated Answer

You can reset your password using the Forgot Password option available on the Application Portal.

Application Portal:
https://applications.bitspilani-digital.edu.in/

A password reset link will be sent to your registered email address.

If you do not receive the email, please check your Spam or Junk folder.

If the issue persists, please contact the Admissions Team and include a screenshot of any error message, if available.

 

Q3. I cannot access my application.

Updated Answer

This may occur due to one of the following reasons:

  • Using a different registered email address.
  • Incorrect password.
  • Browser-related issues.
  • Temporary technical interruption.

Please try the following:

  • Log in again through the official Application Portal:
    https://applications.bitspilani-digital.edu.in/
  • Reset your password using the Forgot Password option.
  • Use the latest version of Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies.
  • Try another device or internet connection.

If you continue to experience difficulties, please email the Admissions Team with a screenshot of the error for further assistance.

 

Q4. Is my application saved automatically?

Updated Answer

Yes.

The application saves completed sections as you progress.

You may resume your application by logging in again through the official Application Portal before the applicable application deadline.

Application Portal:
https://applications.bitspilani-digital.edu.in/

 

Q5. My payment failed.

Updated Answer

For Degree Programmes

An application fee is payable as part of the application submission process.

If your payment was unsuccessful:

  • Wait for approximately 15–30 minutes.
  • Log in again to the Application Portal and verify the payment status.
  • If the payment was unsuccessful, you may attempt the payment again.

If the amount has been debited from your account but your application continues to show an unpaid status, please email the Admissions Team with:

  • Application Number
  • Transaction ID
  • Date and Time of Payment
  • Payment Screenshot

The Admissions Team will verify the transaction and guide you further.

For Certificate Programmes

There is no application fee for Certificate Programmes.

 

Q6. Amount deducted but application still shows unpaid.

Updated Answer

Applicable only to Degree Programmes.

In some cases, banking or payment gateway confirmation may take additional time.

Please allow up to 24 hours for the payment status to update.

If the status remains unchanged, please email the Admissions Team with:

  • Application Number
  • Transaction ID
  • Date and Time of Payment
  • Payment Screenshot

The Admissions Team will verify the payment status and assist you accordingly.

 

Q7. Can I pay later?

Updated Answer

Degree Programmes

Yes.

You may complete the application fee payment any time before the published application deadline, provided admissions remain open.

Please note that your application can only be processed after successful submission and payment of the applicable application fee.

Certificate Programmes

No application fee is required to submit an application.

 

Q8. Which documents do I need?

Updated Answer

The documents required depend on the programme you are applying for.

These may include:

  • Academic mark sheets
  • Degree certificate (if available)
  • Resume (where applicable)
  • Government-issued identity proof (if requested)
  • Experience documents (where applicable)

The Application Portal will indicate the documents required for your programme.

Additional documents may be requested during the admissions verification process, if necessary.

 

Q9. My document upload is failing.

Updated Answer

Please verify the following before uploading:

  • The document is in the prescribed file format.
  • The file size is within the permitted limit.
  • The file is not password protected.
  • Your internet connection is stable.

If the issue persists, please email the Admissions Team with:

  • The document(s) you are trying to upload.
  • A screenshot of the upload error.
  • Your Application Number.

The Admissions Team will assist you further.

 

Q10. Can I submit after the deadline?

Updated Answer

Applications are accepted only while the admissions window remains open.

Applicants are encouraged to submit their applications well before the published deadline.

BITS Pilani Digital follows a rolling admissions process, and admissions may close once the approved intake capacity has been reached, even if a published deadline has not yet elapsed.

 

Q11. How do I know if my application has been submitted?

Updated Answer

After successfully submitting your application:

  • Your application status will be updated in the Application Portal.
  • A confirmation email is generally sent to your registered email address.
  • Your application becomes eligible for review in accordance with the admissions process applicable to the programme.

Applicants are advised to regularly monitor both the Application Portal and their registered email for further updates.

 

Q12. When will my application be reviewed?

Updated Answer

Applications are reviewed after successful submission in accordance with the admissions process applicable to the respective programme.

For Degree Programmes, the applicable application fee must also be successfully paid before the application becomes eligible for review.

Applications are evaluated on a rolling basis, and review timelines may vary depending on the volume of applications received.

 

Q13. I submitted my application but have not received an update.

Updated Answer

Please continue to monitor:

  • The Application Portal:
    https://applications.bitspilani-digital.edu.in/
  • Your registered email address.

If you believe an unusually long period has elapsed without any communication, please contact the Admissions Team quoting your Application Number.

 

Q14. The page keeps loading.

Updated Answer

Please try the following:

  • Refresh the page.
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies.
  • Use Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
  • Try another internet connection.
  • Log in again through the official Application Portal.

If the issue continues, please email the Admissions Team with a screenshot of the error so that the issue can be investigated.

 

Q15. OTP not received.

Updated Answer

Please:

  • Wait a few minutes.
  • Verify that you are using your registered email address or mobile number.
  • Check your Spam or Junk folder (for email OTPs).
  • Request a new OTP.

If you continue to experience the issue, please email the Admissions Team with a screenshot of the error and your registered email address.

 

Q16. Website is slow.

Updated Answer

Temporary delays may occasionally occur during periods of high application activity.

Please wait a few minutes and try again.

If the issue persists, please email the Admissions Team with a screenshot of the issue for further assistance.
