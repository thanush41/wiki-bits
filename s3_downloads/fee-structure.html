7. Bridge / Preparedness Programme Fees 

 

Important Programme Distinction 

A. B.S. in Data Science & AI – Mathematics Bridge Programme 

For the B.S. in Data Science & AI programme, the Mathematics Bridge Programme acts as an academic pathway option for students who do not currently meet the applicable Mathematics eligibility criteria. 

Upon successful completion of the Mathematics Bridge Programme and the applicable academic requirements, eligible learners may progress into the B.S. programme. 

Important: 

This pathway is specifically linked to the Mathematics eligibility requirement for the B.S. programme 

The Bridge Programme is intended to strengthen foundational mathematical readiness prior to progression into the degree programme 

 

B. M.Sc. in Data Science & AI – Preparedness Programmes 

For the M.Sc. in Data Science & AI programme, the preparedness programmes are: 

Mathematics Preparedness Programme 

Python / Programming Preparedness Programme 

Important: 

These programmes are optional in nature 

These programmes act only as refresher/foundation strengthening modules 

These programmes do NOT change or substitute the programme eligibility criteria 

Admissions eligibility decisions remain subject to Admissions Committee evaluation 

These programmes are separate from the degree curriculum and do not carry academic credits or standalone certificates 

 

General Counselor Notes 

These are separate preparatory/refresher programme fees 

These fees are additional to programme tuition fees 

These programmes do not carry academic credits or separate certificates 

These programmes are intended only for strengthening foundational readiness

10. Common Student Queries & Suggested Responses 

Query: Are Bridge Programme fees included in the tuition fee? 

“No. Bridge/Preparedness Programme fees are separate optional fees and are additional to the programme tuition fee.” 

 

Query: Will the Bridge Programme provide credits/certificates?
“No. These are optional preparedness/refresher programmes and do not carry academic credits or standalone certificates.”
