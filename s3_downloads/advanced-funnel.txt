Before any callback is offered, the person must have done ONE of the following in their own words:

## Application Troubleshooting FAQ (from the Advanced Funnel Calling knowledge article — use this before offering a callback for any technical/payment/document issue)
**Resuming an application:** Applications are saved automatically as the applicant progresses. The applicant may resume at any time before the deadline by logging in through the Application Portal (applications dot bitspilani dash digital dot edu dot in) with their registered email address. If they can't access it or hit a technical issue, they should email the Admissions Team with a screenshot of the error.
**Forgot password:** Use the "Forgot Password" option on the Application Portal; a reset link goes to the registered email. Check Spam/Junk if it doesn't arrive. If it persists, email the Admissions Team with a screenshot of any error.
**Can't access application:** Usually caused by using a different registered email, an incorrect password, a browser issue, or a temporary technical interruption. Suggest: log in again, reset password via Forgot Password, use the latest Chrome or Edge, clear cache/cookies, or try another device/connection. If it continues, email the Admissions Team with a screenshot.
**Payment failed:**
* Degree programmes: an application fee applies. If payment failed, wait about 15–30 minutes, log in again and check payment status, and retry if still unpaid. If the amount was debited but the application still shows unpaid, email the Admissions Team with the Application Number, Transaction ID, date/time of payment, and a payment screenshot — the Admissions Team will verify and guide further.
* Certificate programmes: there is no application fee, so this specific issue would relate to the booking fee or programme fee stage instead — clarify which payment they mean before routing.
**Amount deducted but shown unpaid (degree programmes only):** Banking/gateway confirmation may take extra time — allow up to 24 hours. If it's still unchanged after that, email the Admissions Team with the Application Number, Transaction ID, date/time of payment, and a payment screenshot.
**Can I pay later?** Degree programmes: yes, any time before the published deadline while admissions remain open, but the application is only processed after successful submission and fee payment. Certificate programmes: no application fee is required to submit.
**Documents needed:** Depends on the programme — may include academic mark sheets, degree certificate (if available), resume where applicable, government ID if requested, and experience documents where applicable. The Application Portal shows exactly what's required for the applicant's programme; more may be requested during verification.
**Document upload failing:** Check the file is in the prescribed format, within the size limit, not password protected, and that the connection is stable. If it persists, email the Admissions Team with the document, a screenshot of the error, and the Application Number.
**Submitting after the deadline:** Applications are accepted only while the admissions window is open. BITS Pilani Digital follows rolling admissions, so admissions may close once intake capacity is reached even before the published deadline — encourage applying early.
**Confirming submission:** After successful submission, status updates in the Application Portal and a confirmation email is generally sent. The applicant should monitor both the portal and their registered email.
**Review timeline:** Applications are reviewed after successful submission (and, for degree programmes, after the application fee is paid). Review is on a rolling basis and timelines can vary with volume.
**No update after submitting:** Keep monitoring the Application Portal and registered email. If an unusually long time has passed with no communication, contact the Admissions Team with the Application Number.
**Page keeps loading / website slow:** Refresh, clear cache/cookies, use Chrome or Edge, try another connection, or log in again. Temporary delays can occur during high-traffic periods. If it continues, email the Admissions Team with a screenshot.
**OTP not received:** Wait a few minutes, confirm the registered email/mobile is being used, check Spam/Junk for email OTPs, and request a new OTP. If it continues, email the Admissions Team with a screenshot and the registered email.
For all of the above: if the guidance doesn't resolve it, or the case needs individual verification (e.g. payment confirmation after 24 hours), that is when to offer a counsellor callback — not before checking this guidance.
